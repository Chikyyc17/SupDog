OUR STORY

BEHIND THE FLAVOR

Sup Dog was born as much more than just an idea; it was born from a need to express who we truly are.

Behind every dish stands Jonathan Sotelo, our creative soul. With over 18 years in the industry, Jonathan has explored diverse culinary cultures, gathering experiences that he now pours into every recipe. For him, Sup Dog is just the beginning—a future built on tireless effort, absolute dedication, and above all, walking hand-in-hand with God.

But every great vision needs a driving force, and that is where María Colmenarez comes in. She is the perfect match—a tireless woman who always strives for more. She was the key to propelling this dream forward, turning the Chef's experience and technique into a tangible reality.

For us, Sup Dog is our way of communicating. It represents an informal, genuine, and authentic connection. Because at the end of the day, Sup Dog isn't just a greeting; it is an invitation to be yourself.

Jonathan & María
